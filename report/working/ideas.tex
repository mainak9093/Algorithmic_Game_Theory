\section{Open Threads: Status Register}

Every thread this investigation has raised is listed once below, with a status.
The vocabulary is fixed:

\begin{description}\itemsep2pt
  \item[\textsc{proved}] a theorem of this report.
  \item[\textsc{refuted}] a counterexample or machine-verified failure exists.
  \item[\textsc{withdrawn}] asserted here, then falsified by later work here.
  \item[\textsc{open}] precisely stated, unrefuted, and load-bearing.
  \item[\textsc{unexplored}] stated, never tested.
  \item[\textsc{superseded}] absorbed into a later, stronger formulation.
  \item[\textsc{excluded}] deliberately out of scope.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Out of scope}

Binary submodular / matroid-rank valuations, and the milestone ladder through
them, are \textsc{excluded}: the dichotomous \emph{goods} corner is already
settled in~\cite{BKNS22} by matroid machinery, and retreating to that hypothesis
would re-prove a known result rather than the open one --- see
Remark~\ref{rem:submodular-is-the-gap} for why submodularity is exactly what this
problem lacks. The mixed goods-and-chores model of~\cite{KMSTY24} is likewise
\textsc{excluded} as a different problem.

\subsection*{What the closed column means}

The refutations above are not independent. Grouped by the technique they kill:
constructive routes fail because the witness exists but no local rule finds it
(\S\ref{sec:approach7-obstruction}, \S\ref{sec:approach8-induction});
decompositional routes fail because every two-term split of $\pathw{\alloc}$ has
both parts unbounded while the whole stays small (Rem.~\ref{rem:cancellation},
four instances); transplanted routes fail because additivity is load-bearing
in~\cite{BDNSV20} and submodularity in~\cite{LMS26}
(\S\ref{sec:approach6-bdnsv-audit}, \S\ref{sec:approach6-r11-audit}); and
non-local routes fail for want of a distribution or an identity
(\S\ref{sec:approach8-nonlocal}). Every named technique class has now been tried.
That is a statement about where a proof will not come from, not evidence that
Conjecture~\ref{conj:main} is false --- it survives every test at every size
reached, now to $m = 12$.
